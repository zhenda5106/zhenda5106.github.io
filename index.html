About me

I am a first-year undergraduate student at the University of Toronto studying Mathematical and Physical Sciences, with the intention of entering the Physics Specialist and Mathematics Major programs.

My developing interests lie in fundamental theoretical physics, particularly questions concerning physics beyond the Standard Model, quantum gravity, spacetime, and the mathematical structures used to describe physical systems. I am currently building the necessary foundation through coursework in physics, mathematics, and programming.

I am also beginning to explore computational methods through an AI-assisted numerical project on the double pendulum. At this stage, my goal is to learn carefully, gain undergraduate research experience, and gradually identify the theoretical questions I am best prepared to pursue.
