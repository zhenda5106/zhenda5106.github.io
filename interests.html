Questions I am exploring

Academic &
Research Interests

I am currently exploring several directions in fundamental theoretical physics while building a stronger foundation in physics and mathematics. These are developing interests and long-term learning goals rather than established research specialties.

Primary Developing Interests

Fundamental High-Energy Theory

I am especially interested in theoretical questions that go beyond the Standard Model of particle physics. I would like to understand how new mathematical and physical principles might address unresolved questions about fundamental particles, interactions, and the structure of physical law.

Quantum Gravity and Spacetime

I am strongly curious about the relationship between quantum theory and gravity, including the nature of spacetime and the theoretical description of black holes. These are long-term interests that I hope to approach after developing a rigorous foundation in classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, relativity, and mathematics.

Areas I Hope to Explore

Quantum Foundations

I am interested in the conceptual and mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics, including what the theory tells us about measurement, physical reality, and the limits of classical descriptions.

Quantum Field Theory and Mathematical Physics

Quantum field theory is a major long-term learning goal. I am interested in how mathematical structures are used to describe fields, particles, symmetries, and interactions, while recognizing that I must first build the required background in mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, linear algebra, and analysis.

Related Interests

Cosmology, Dark Matter, and Particle Phenomenology

I would also like to learn how fundamental theories connect with observations through theoretical cosmology, dark matter models, and particle phenomenology. These areas interest me as possible bridges between abstract theory and experimentally testable physics.

Experimental Connections

Although my strongest current interests are theoretical, I am also interested in understanding how experiments, data analysis, and instrumentation constrain physical theories. I am open to gaining undergraduate experience in computational or experimental work as part of developing a broader understanding of physics.
