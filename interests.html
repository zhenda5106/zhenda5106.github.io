Questions I am exploring

Academic &
Research Interests

I am currently exploring possible directions in theoretical and particle physics and hope to gain undergraduate research experience as I develop a stronger foundation in physics and mathematics. These are areas of curiosity and learning, rather than established research specialties.

Theoretical Physics

I am interested in how mathematical models describe fundamental physical systems. Cosmology is one area I would like to learn more about as I develop the tools to understand theoretical descriptions of the universe.

High-Energy Physics

I would like to explore particle physics and the theoretical ideas used to describe fundamental particles and their interactions. Quantum field theory is a longer-term learning interest as I strengthen my background in physics and mathematics.

Quantum Physics

I am interested in learning the principles of quantum mechanics and how its mathematical framework describes physical systems. My focus is on building the foundations needed to approach more advanced questions.

Mathematical Physics

I am curious about the relationship between mathematical structures and physical ideas. Alongside coursework, I am exploring how numerical methods can help investigate systems such as the double pendulum.
